Classic Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the wet ingredients:
    • Egg
    • Milk
    • Melted butter
  3. Stir until the batter is combined.
  4. Pour small portions of batter into a heated pan.
  5. Cook both sides until golden brown.
